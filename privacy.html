Privacy policy

This page explains what information wrotron.in collects and how Wrotron Technologies Pvt. Ltd. uses it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, organisation, phone number, email, city, approximate number of people, what you’re looking for, and any message you add.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: whatever you choose to send us through those channels.
  • Website analytics: standard usage information collected by Google Analytics (through Firebase), such as pages visited, device type and approximate location.

How we use it

We use your enquiry details only to respond to you, arrange a site assessment and prepare a proposal. Analytics help us understand how the website is used so we can improve it. We don’t sell your information.

Where it’s stored

Enquiries submitted through the form are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you’ve shared with us, email admin.team@wrotron.in or call +91 89693 09787.

Changes

We may update this page as the website changes. The latest version will always be here.
