FAQ

Straight answers.

Can’t find yours? Ask the team that runs Wrotron.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a vending machine cost us?

Nothing up front. Wrotron funds the machine, installation, stocking and servicing from product sales. There are no setup, rental, maintenance or subscription fees.

Do you sell or rent machines?

Neither. We place and operate the machine ourselves as a managed service. You provide the space; we run everything else.

Do you handle installation?

Yes — delivery, placement, power, payment setup and connection, scheduled at a time that suits you.

Who stocks the machine, and how often?

Our team does. Refills are planned from live stock data rather than a fixed calendar, so busy machines are visited more often. The expected service schedule for your site is set out in your proposal.

Which payment methods are supported?

Machines are cashless: any UPI app (such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or BHIM), debit and credit cards (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard) and NFC tap to pay. No Wrotron app is needed.

What if the machine stops working, or a product doesn’t drop?

Machines report faults to us automatically and our team fixes them. Response and uptime commitments are written into your agreement, with service credits if we miss them. Anyone with a purchase problem — a product that didn’t drop, or a payment that went through twice — can reach us using the contact details on the machine, and we’ll make it right. Your team never has to handle it.

Can we choose the products — including healthier ones?

Yes. You approve the product list and price points before installation, and can ask for changes at any time. Healthier options sit alongside everyday favourites, and the monthly report shows what’s selling.

What if some products don’t sell?

We swap them out. The sales data shows what’s moving and what isn’t, and we change the mix — at no cost to you. Unsold stock is our problem, not yours.

What will people pay?

Packaged products are sold at or below their printed MRP — never above.

Can the machine be branded?

Co-branding options are available on the machine. We’ll show you what’s possible for your site in the proposal.

How much space and electricity does it need?

A spot where people pass or gather, a standard power socket nearby and mobile network coverage. Exact dimensions and power requirements depend on the machine we recommend, and we confirm them during the site assessment.

Is maintenance included, or is there an AMC?

Servicing, cleaning and repairs are part of the managed service. There’s no separate maintenance contract to buy.

Can you support multiple locations?

Yes. We work with organisations across India. Tell us about each location in the enquiry form or on WhatsApp and we’ll plan them together.

How quickly can a machine be installed?

After the site assessment and agreement, we schedule installation at a time that suits you. We’ll give you a date in the proposal.

Are we locked into a contract?

No lock-in — we earn your business every month. Notice terms are set out clearly in your agreement.

You’re a young company — why should we trust you?

A fair question. We put our commitments in a written service agreement with service credits, and share company registration, GST and FSSAI compliance documents on request.
