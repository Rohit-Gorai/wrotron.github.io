The Wrotron Story

One IIM grad.
One broken industry.
One big mission.

India has over 10 million offices, hospitals, factories and campuses. Almost none of them have reliable, modern vending. Workers queue for chai. Hospital staff skip meals. Campus students can't find a snack at midnight.

Wrotron was founded to fix exactly this. We're not just selling machines — we're building the infrastructure layer that makes Indian workplaces more productive, more comfortable, and more modern.

We're early. We're growing fast. And we want you with us. Every organisation that partners with Wrotron today isn't just getting a vending machine — they're backing an idea whose time has come.
