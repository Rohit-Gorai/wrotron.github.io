About Wrotron

IIM-bred insight.
Ground-level execution.

Wrotron was built on a simple observation: India's offices, hospitals and campuses deserve the same quality of vending infrastructure that global organisations take for granted — reliable machines, cashless payments, real-time monitoring, and a partner that actually shows up.

Founded by an IIM Sambalpur MBA and ex-GCP engineer, Wrotron combines strategic rigour with hands-on operations. We're not a distributor — we're an operator. Every machine we place is our responsibility, not yours.
