FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Can’t find yours? Ask us directly — you’ll hear back from the team that runs Wrotron.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is there really no cost to our organisation?

Yes. Wrotron funds the machine, installation, stocking and servicing from product sales. There are no setup, maintenance or subscription fees. High-footfall locations can also opt for a revenue share.

What do you need from us?

A spot where people pass or gather, a standard power point nearby, mobile network coverage at that spot, and access for our team when we restock and service. That’s it.

What will our people pay?

Packaged products are sold at or below their printed MRP — never above. You approve the product list and price points before we install.

Which payment methods are supported?

Machines are fully cashless: any UPI app (such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or BHIM), debit and credit cards (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard) and NFC tap to pay.

What if a machine breaks down, or a product doesn’t drop?

Machines report faults to us automatically and our team is dispatched to fix them. Response and uptime commitments are written into your agreement, with service credits if we miss them. If anyone has a problem with a purchase, our contact details are on the machine and we’ll make it right.

You’re a young company — why should we trust you?

A fair question. Every partnership matters to our reputation, so we put our commitments in a written service agreement with service credits. GST registration, FSSAI compliance documents and company registration (MCA/ROC) details are available on request.

Do you serve our city?

We serve organisations across India. Share your city and PIN code in the enquiry form and we’ll plan the survey and installation for your location.

How does installation work?

After the site survey and agreement, we schedule installation at a time that suits you. We handle placement, power, payment activation and cloud connection without disrupting your operations.

Can we choose what goes into the machine?

Absolutely. We build the product mix with you — preferred brands, dietary needs and price points — and you can ask for changes at any time. The monthly report shows what’s selling to guide those choices.

Are we locked into a contract?

No. There’s no lock-in — we earn your business every month. Notice terms are set out clearly in your agreement.
