The Wrotron Story

One problem.
One IIM grad.
One big mission.

India has over 10 million offices, hospitals, factories and campuses. Almost none of them have reliable, modern vending. Workers queue for chai. Hospital staff skip meals. Campus students can’t find a snack at midnight. The machines that exist are cash-only, poorly stocked, and never maintained.

Wrotron was founded to fix this — from the ground up. We’re building the smart vending infrastructure layer that Indian workplaces have deserved for decades: cashless, IoT-connected, always-stocked, always-on.

We’re early. We’re growing fast. Every organisation that partners with Wrotron today isn’t just getting a machine — they’re backing an idea whose time has finally come.
