FAQ

Questions,
answered honestly.

Is there really no cost to our organisation?

Yes. Wrotron funds the machine, installation, restocking and maintenance from product sales. There are no setup, maintenance or subscription fees. High-footfall locations can also opt for a revenue share.

You’re a young company — why should we trust you?

A fair question. Every partnership matters to our reputation, so we put our commitments in a written service agreement with service credits. GST registration, FSSAI compliance documents and company registration (MCA/ROC) details are available on request.

Do you serve our city?

We serve organisations across India. Share your city and PIN code in the enquiry form and we’ll plan the survey and installation for your location.

What happens if a machine breaks down?

Machines report faults to us automatically, and our team is dispatched to fix them. Response and uptime commitments are written into your agreement, with service credits if we miss them — you don’t have to ask.

How does installation work?

After the site survey and agreement, we schedule installation at a time that suits you. We handle placement, power, payment activation and cloud connection without disrupting your operations.

Can we choose what goes into the machine?

Absolutely. We build the product mix with you — preferred brands, dietary requirements and price points — and you can request changes at any time. The monthly report shows what’s selling to guide those choices.

Which payment methods are supported?

Machines are fully cashless: UPI (any UPI app, such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or BHIM), debit and credit cards (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard), and NFC tap to pay.
