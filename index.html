FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Wrotron funds the machine, installation, stocking and servicing from product sales. There are no setup, rental, maintenance or subscription fees — and no machine to buy.

What do you need from us?

A spot where people pass or gather, a standard power socket nearby, mobile network coverage at that spot, and access for our team to restock and service. That’s it.

What will people pay?

Packaged products are sold at or below their printed MRP — never above. You approve the product list and price points before we install.

Which payments are supported?

Any UPI app (such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm or BHIM), debit and credit cards (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard) and NFC tap to pay. No Wrotron app is needed.

What happens if the machine breaks?

Machines report faults to us and our team fixes them. Response and uptime commitments are written into your agreement, with service credits if we miss them. If a product doesn’t drop or a payment goes through twice, the person can reach us using the details on the machine and we’ll make it right — your team never has to handle it.

How is stock managed?

Our team restocks the machine. Refills are planned from live stock data rather than a fixed calendar, so busy machines are visited more often. Products that don’t sell are swapped out at no cost to you.

Can we choose the products?

Yes — including healthier options. You approve the list before installation and can ask for changes any time. The monthly report shows what’s selling to guide those choices.

Is there a contract?

There’s a written service agreement that sets out what we commit to. There’s no lock-in — we earn your business every month, and notice terms are clear in the agreement.

Do you serve our city?

We work with organisations across India. Share your city in the form and we’ll plan the survey and installation for your location. Several locations? Tell us about each and we’ll plan them together.

How does installation work?

After the site survey and agreement, we schedule installation at a time that suits you and handle placement, power, payment setup and connection. You’ll get a date in the proposal.

Is maintenance included, or is there an AMC?

Servicing, cleaning and repairs are part of the managed service. There’s no separate maintenance contract to buy.

How much space and power does it need?

A spot where people pass or gather and a standard power socket. Exact dimensions and power requirements depend on the machine we recommend — we confirm them during the site survey.

Can the machine carry our branding?

Co-branding options are available. We’ll show you what’s possible for your site in the proposal.

You’re a young company — why trust you?

A fair question. We put our commitments in a written service agreement with service credits, and share company registration, GST and FSSAI compliance documents on request.
